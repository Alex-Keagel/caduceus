## Findings

### 🔴 Critical
1. [`docs/specs/spec-multi-repo-workspace-model.md:777-785`](../docs/specs/spec-multi-repo-workspace-model.md) — The `OrphanReclaim` bypass is mis-scoped and mis-targeted: it says queued rows “proceed directly … step 7”, which would skip §3.6 steps 5/5a/6 if implemented literally, and it only covers §5B.2 step-4 queue entries, leaving positively-disproved `OrphanPending` rows able to loop back to `OrphanPending`. **Fix:**

```md
**OrphanReclaim liveness-bypass (normative).** Any `cleanup_workspace(_, OrphanReclaim)` invocation whose caller has already reached a **positively disproved** liveness verdict MUST bypass ONLY this step 4 layered liveness probe and continue at step 5 (parent revalidation + `before_cleanup` hook). Steps 5–9 remain mandatory. This applies both to rows drained from the `OrphanReclaim` queue (§5B.2 step 4 / step 5) and to `OrphanPending` rows that reconcile has just reclassified from inconclusive to positively disproved (§5B.1 row `OrphanPending`). Such invocations MUST NOT transition the row back to `Status::OrphanPending`; otherwise reconcile can livelock between `OrphanPending` and cleanup.
```

### 🟡 Important
1. [`docs/specs/spec-multi-repo-workspace-model.md:1585`](../docs/specs/spec-multi-repo-workspace-model.md) — Dead-`Active` is classified two different ways: direct `cleanup_workspace(_, OrphanReclaim)` here, but queued `OrphanReclaim` in §5B.2 step 4 and §4.3 line 1296. **Fix:**

```md
| `Active` | leaf exists; runner may be alive | LIVE-OR-ORPHAN | §3.6 step 4 layered liveness probe (heartbeat file + pid+pgrp). Alive ⇒ leave `Active`. Inconclusive ⇒ transition to `OrphanPending`. Positively disproved ⇒ enqueue `OrphanReclaim { slug, run_id }`; the row remains `Active` until reconcile drains that queue per §5B.2 step 7 and dispatches `cleanup_workspace(_, OrphanReclaim)`. |
```

2. [`docs/specs/spec-multi-repo-workspace-model.md:1290`](../docs/specs/spec-multi-repo-workspace-model.md) — The `Creating` state row still says startup/reconcile positive disproof exits through `OrphanPending`; §5B.2 step 4 says positive disproof enqueues `OrphanReclaim` while the row remains `Creating` until queue drain. **Fix:**

```md
| `Creating` | `create_workspace` (§3.5) step 1.4 inserts the placeholder row | `Active` (on step 9 success); `OrphanPending` (startup/reconcile crash-recovery when layered liveness is inconclusive, per §5B.2 step 4); `OrphanReclaim`-queue while the row remains `Creating` (startup/reconcile crash-recovery when layered liveness is positively disproved, per §5B.2 step 4); on step 10 rollback the row is **removed iff rollback reaches step 10c**, otherwise retained as `Status::CleanupFailed` if rollback aborts at `Error::CleanupOwnershipFailed` (step 10a-pre), `Error::ParentRevalidationFailed` (step 10a), or mid-walk unlinkat failure (step 10e) per §3.5 step 1b | The runner MUST NOT bind cwd while status is `Creating`. |
```

3.
