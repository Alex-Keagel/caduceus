## Findings

### 🔴 Critical
1. [§3.4 L919-947] — Z-29 defines `span == min(current_stream_seq + 1, ring_size)` and dense retention over `[ring_oldest_seq, current_stream_seq]`, then also claims replay-index emptiness is natural at `current_stream_seq == 0`. These are mutually inconsistent unless the invariant is explicitly conditional for boot-empty state.
   **Fix:** Replace the invariant block with conditional language that defines an explicit boot-empty case and only applies span/dense-retention when `current_stream_seq > 0`.

2. [§6 T-31a L2306-2310 vs §3.4 L949-951] — T-31a requires payload-buffer eviction while index still contains entries, but Z-29 sub-invariant 3 forbids index/buffer divergence at any point. Test as written is impossible under the normative algorithm.
   **Fix:** Rewrite T-31a to represent a legal cancellation path (e.g., replay payload missing at replay-read boundary due corruption/restart/rebuild) without asserting index/buffer mismatch under lock-step trim.

### 🟡 Important
1. [§6 T-31b L2318-2320] — “trim MUST NOT proceed until subscriber receives cancel frame” is not implementable in at-most-once transport; daemon cannot observe remote receipt.
   **Fix:** Replace with enqueue/ordering semantics observable by daemon (enqueue cancel frame, close queue, no further frames; trim commit atomic with cancellation decision).

2. [§3.4 L737-740] — “collision in clause (a)” wording is misleading; this paragraph is about pre-v1 clause-(b) fallback detection limits.
   **Fix:** Rename to “collision risk in pre-v1 cross-incarnation detection fallback” and avoid implying clause-(a) ownership.

### 🟢 Suggestions
1. [§3.4 L451, §6 L1976] — “delta stream” phrasing may be read as ignoring `SubscribeFrame` envelope.
   **Fix:** Prefer “subscribe stream (`Stream<SubscribeFrame>`) with post-ack delta frames” in these two lines.

## Recommended Actions
1. Normalize Z-29 with an explicit boot-empty branch and adjusted dense-retention domain.
2. Reconcile T-31a with lock-step invariant (or relax invariant explicitly, but that would ripple into safety proofs).
3. Make T-31b acceptance criterion daemon-observable (enqueue/close ordering, not client receipt).
