### 🟡 Important

1. **L397 — parenthetical mislabels step 8.5 as the placeholder-row insert.**
   The current text reads: *"Steps 3–8 (and step 8.5, the placeholder-row insert) on failure enter the rollback path at step 10."*
   Step 8.5 is the **leaf-ownership handoff** (L549, §5A.5), NOT the placeholder-row insert. The placeholder row is inserted at **step 1.4** (per L589, L665, L1159, L1274, L1568, L1643). The appositive corrupts the meaning of the rollback obligation paragraph and contradicts five other sites in the spec.
   **Fix:** replace the opening sentence of §3.5 step 1b with:
   ```
   1b. **Rollback obligation.** Steps 3–8 and step 8.5 (leaf-ownership handoff) on failure enter the rollback path at step 10. The placeholder row inserted at step 1.4 is the subject of the rollback's registry-cleanup sub-step (10c). Rollback may abort at step 10a-pre (`CleanupOwnershipFailed`), step 10a (`ParentRevalidationFailed`), or step 10e mid-walk unlinkat failure (`CleanupIncomplete (rollback-side)`); in those cases the row and leaf are retained as `Status::CleanupFailed` for reconcile.
   ```

2. **§3.5 step 10e (L673–678) — prose omits the named error symbol that the taxonomy at L1307 sources to this step.**
   Step 10a-pre and step 10a both name their side-channel error ("`Error::CleanupOwnershipFailed`" / "`Error::ParentRevalidationFailed`"). Step 10e currently says only "transitioned to `Status::CleanupFailed`" without naming `Error::CleanupIncomplete (rollback-side)`, even though the error taxonomy at L1307 explicitly sources `CleanupIncomplete (rollback-side)` to "§3.5 step 10e". This makes step 10e the only one of three rollback-abort paths whose prose doesn't pin its own error name — a reader landing in step 10 cannot find where `CleanupIncomplete (rollback-side)` is produced.
   **Fix:** replace the second sentence of step 10e with:
   ```
   If the leaf walk fails partway, the daemon MUST emit `Error::CleanupIncomplete` (rollback-side) as the side-channel diagnostic, transition the registry row to `Status::CleanupFailed`, and rely on reconcile (§4.3) to resume the cleanup later. The original triggering error (e.g. `Error::HookFailed`) remains the value returned to the caller per Invariant I-7; `CleanupIncomplete (rollback-side)` is logged but not returned.
   ```

3. **§5B.3 diagram (L1639–1679) — Active→CleaningUp "liveness disproved" edge is not drawn; two distinct labels stack on a single visual pipe ending at OrphanPending.**
   The leftmost vertical pipe carries the label *"liveness disproved (reconcile)"* at L1651–1654 and *"liveness inconclusive (cleanup-time OrphanReclaim)"* at L1672–1675, then terminates at OrphanPending (L1677). Per §5B.1 (L1570), Active→CleaningUp on liveness-disproved and Active→OrphanPending on liveness-inconclusive are **two distinct transitions**. The diagram conflates them onto one arrow and only renders the OrphanPending terminus, so a reader of the (informative) diagram may believe Active→OrphanPending is the only path and that disproved-liveness also flows there. The §3.6 step 1 arrow Active→CleaningUp does exist (L1653) but it represents `cleanup_workspace` invocation by the orchestrator, NOT reconcile-driven liveness-disproved transitions.
   **Fix:** add an explicit reconcile-driven Active→CleaningUp arrow (or merge the two reconcile-driven Active outflows into a fork node):
   ```
                ┌──────────────┐
                │    Active    │
                └──┬─────┬─────┘
                   │     │ liveness disproved (reconcile §3.6 step 4)
   §3.6 step 1 ────┘     ▼
   (orchestrator   ┌──────────────┐
    invocation)    │  CleaningUp  │
                   └──────────────┘
                           ▲
                           │ liveness positively disproved
                   ┌───────┴──────┐
                   │ OrphanPending│◄──── liveness inconclusive
                   └──────────────┘      (Active, reconcile §3.6 step 4)
   ```
   Or, if the existing layout is to be preserved, split the leftmost pipe into two clearly-routed arrows: one terminating at CleaningUp (labeled "liveness disproved (reconcile)") and one terminating at OrphanPending (labeled "liveness inconclusive (reconcile)"). The current single-pipe rendering with two stacked labels is the source of the ambiguity.
