## Findings

### 🔴 Critical
1. [docs/specs/spec-orchestrator-status-snapshot.md:440-446] — `Stream protocol` still says `UpToDate` means the subscriber's `since_fingerprint` matches the daemon's **current** fingerprint. That contradicts clause (d), where `UpToDate` is legal precisely when the subscriber matches `fp_at(s_c)` but differs from `current_fingerprint`. **Fix:** rewrite this paragraph so `UpToDate` means “no full resync is needed”; vacuous shape matches current fingerprint, clause-(d) shape uses replay from `s_c`.
2. [docs/specs/spec-orchestrator-status-snapshot.md:507-508] — `First-delta ordering` still says `SubscribeAck` MUST carry the daemon's current `stream_seq`, which conflicts with clause-(d) and §3.4.1 where `ack.stream_seq = s_c`. **Fix:** scope the rule: Resync + vacuous UpToDate carry current; clause-(d) UpToDate+replay carries `s_c`.
3. [docs/specs/spec-orchestrator-status-snapshot.md:530-533] — clause-(d) summary says the first delta after the ack MAY be any frame in `(s_c, current]`. That permits out-of-order replay and conflicts with the replay-window semantics. **Fix:** say the first replay delta has `stream_seq = s_c + 1`, then replay proceeds monotonically through `current`.
