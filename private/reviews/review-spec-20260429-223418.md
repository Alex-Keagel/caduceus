## Findings

### 🔴 Critical
1. [docs/specs/spec-multi-repo-workspace-model.md:912-914] — §3.6 step 4 still says `four enqueue sources`, but §5B.2 step 7 canonically enumerates **five** sources at lines 1964-1967 and states that any divergence is a defect. This is a normative self-contradiction. **Fix:**
   ```md
   Rows reaching this step via the `OrphanReclaim` queue (any of the
   five enqueue sources enumerated in §5B.2 step 7) MUST skip ONLY the
   layered liveness probe of this step 4; steps 5 (parent revalidation),
   5a (ownership reclaim), 6 (path re-validation), 7 (recursive
   remove), 8 (`after_cleanup`), and 9 (lock release) MUST run
   unchanged.
   ```

### 🟡 Important
1. [docs/specs/spec-multi-repo-workspace-model.md:2013-2019, 1469-1472, 1764-1766] — `CleanupFailed` is said to receive bounded reconcile retry, but the spec still does not say how that retry preserves the queue-only reclaim invariant or the `CleanupFailed -> CleaningUp` transition required by the state table. An implementer can reasonably retry cleanup directly from `CleanupFailed`, which conflicts with lines 1764-1766 and the `CleanupFailed` exit in line 1472. **Fix:**
   ```md
   Independently, the same reconcile pass re-evaluates rows in
   `OrphanPending` and `CleanupFailed` per §5B.1. For `OrphanPending`,
   reconcile re-runs §3.6 step 4 layered liveness; when liveness becomes
   positively disproved, reconcile MUST enqueue `OrphanReclaim { slug,
   run_id }` rather than directly calling `cleanup_workspace`, so the
   queue remains the single canonical reclaim path. For `CleanupFailed`,
   bounded retry is ALSO routed through `OrphanReclaim`: if the row's
   retry budget is not exhausted, reconcile MUST enqueue
   `OrphanReclaim { slug, run_id }`, and the queue drain's B23 lifecycle
   transition performs the required `CleanupFailed -> CleaningUp`
   mutation before re-entering §3.6 at step 3. Direct destructive retry
   from `CleanupFailed` outside the queue drain is FORBIDDEN.
   ```
2. [docs/specs/spec-multi-repo-workspace-model.md:1930-1944] — §5B.2 step 7(a) still mislabels §3.6 step 2 as `row load`, and the parenthetical `the §5B.2 step 4 source #5 startup-recovery case` is ambiguous because `source #5` is defined in step 7's canonical enumeration, not inside step 4. **Fix:**
   ```md
   (a) **Start at step 3** — skip §3.6 step 1 (per-workspace lock
       acquisition AND the `→ CleaningUp` row-state transition) and
       §3.6 step 2 (path re-validation). The queue entry already
       provides `(slug, run_id)`, and the reconciler holds the per-slug
       lock per §4.5; the per-workspace lock is acquired by the drain
       itself before invoking §3.6 step 3.

       **Lifecycle transition (B23; normative).** Before invoking
       §3.6 step 3, the drain MUST perform the state mutation normally
       owned by §3.6 step 1: if `registry_row.is_some()` and the row's
       current `Status` is not already `CleaningUp`, briefly re-acquire
       the registry-wide mutex and transition the row to
       `Status::CleaningUp`, then release the mutex and continue at
       step 3. (Rows that are already `CleaningUp` — i.e., source #5 in
       §5B.2 step 7's canonical enumeration, corresponding to the
       §5B.2 step 4 `CleaningUp` startup-recovery case — skip the
       transition and proceed directly to step 3.)
   ```
