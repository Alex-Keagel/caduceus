## Findings

### 🔴 Critical
1. [spec-multi-repo-workspace-model.md:777-785] — `OrphanReclaim` bypass is wrong in two ways: it says "proceed directly ... step 7," which skips mandatory safety steps 5/5a/6, and it only covers the startup queue path, not the `OrphanPending` -> positively-disproved path, so reconcile can still loop back to `OrphanPending`.
   **Fix:** Replace the paragraph with:
   
   ```md
   **OrphanReclaim liveness-bypass (normative).** Any `cleanup_workspace(_, OrphanReclaim)` invocation whose caller has already reached a **positively disproved** liveness verdict MUST bypass ONLY this step 4 layered liveness probe and continue at §3.6 step 5 (parent revalidation + `before_cleanup` hook). Steps 5–9 remain mandatory. This applies both to rows queued by §5B.2 step 4 and to `OrphanPending` rows that reconcile has just reclassified from inconclusive to positively disproved. Such invocations MUST NOT transition the row back to `Status::OrphanPending`; otherwise reconcile can livelock between `OrphanPending` and cleanup.
   ```

2. [spec-multi-repo-workspace-model.md:1585, 1604-1607, 1661-1667] — dead-`Active` handling still disagrees across the spec. §5B.1 says disproved `Active` goes straight to `cleanup_workspace(_, OrphanReclaim)`, §5B.2 step 4 says it is only queued and stays `Active`, and step 7 never scans `Active` rows at runtime, so a dead runner after startup can leak forever until restart.
   **Fix:**
   - Replace line 1585 row text with:
   
   ```md
   | `Active` | leaf exists; runner may be alive | LIVE-OR-ORPHAN | §3.6 step 4 layered liveness probe (heartbeat file + pid+pgrp). Alive ⇒ leave `Active`. Inconclusive ⇒ transition to `OrphanPending`. Positively disproved ⇒ enqueue `OrphanReclaim { slug, run_id }`; the row remains `Active` until reconcile drains that queue per §5B.2 step 7 and dispatches `cleanup_workspace(_, OrphanReclaim)`. |
   ```
   
   - Replace step 7 text at 1661-1667 with:
   
   ```md
   7. **Reconcile loop.** A background reconcile task (cadence:
      `2 × heartbeat_interval`, default 10s) iterates `Active`,
      `OrphanPending`, and `CleanupFailed` rows and re-evaluates per §5B.1.
      For `Active` rows, apply the three-way liveness switch from §4.3:
      Alive ⇒ unchanged; Inconclusive ⇒ `OrphanPending`; Positively
      disproved ⇒ enqueue `OrphanReclaim { slug, run_id }`. The loop ALSO
      drains the `OrphanReclaim` queue populated by step 4 (positively-
      disproved `Creating`/`Active` rows) and by step 5 (orphan-leaf scan):
      each queued `OrphanReclaim { slug, run_id }` is dispatched to
      `cleanup_workspace(_, OrphanReclaim)` on the current reconcile pass.
   ```
